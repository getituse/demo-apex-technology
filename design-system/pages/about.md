# About — overrides

Only what differs from [MASTER](../MASTER.md).

**Job of this page:** answer "who are you and why should I trust you" with substance rather than
adjectives. This is the page that converts a sceptical parent or a procurement lead.

## Layout rhythm

**Prose-led, not card-led.** Density `spacious`. Container `narrow` (720px) for all body copy —
this is the one page where measure matters more than filling the viewport. Full-width only for
imagery and the values band.

No hero image with overlaid text. A page title on the canvas, then a lead paragraph, then content.
Credibility comes from what is written, not from a stock photo of a lecture hall.

## Section-specific notes

**About split** — image and prose alternate sides down the page, max three pairs. Images are
`4/3`. Alternation must not create a zigzag on mobile; everything stacks image-first.

**Mission / vision** — two panels on `sectionTint`, equal height, no icons. Text only. Icons here
read as filler.

**Values** — 3–6 items. Icon (Lucide, `accent`), short title, one-sentence body. This is the only
place on the page where a card grid is appropriate.

**Principal / leadership message** — the long form. Portrait at `4/5`, full message beneath in
`narrow` measure, signature block with name and role. Optional pull-quote at `h3` size in `muted`
to break up a long text block.

**Timeline / history** — vertical, date-led, left rail. Optional and off by default; only enable
when the tenant has genuine history to show. Never invent milestones.

## Content discipline

This page attracts fabrication more than any other. Nothing about recognition, affiliation,
ranking or award appears unless it is real and in the tenant's data. Where the layout expects a
credential the tenant does not have, **the section is disabled** — it does not render an empty
frame or a placeholder badge.
